**Milestone 12, magic-link sign-in.** From workspace apply-m12-magic-link. Release-blocking: the sign-in copy ships with the next release.

**What was built:** sign-in by e-mailed link instead of a password. The session had to pick how long a link works, and picked 15 minutes.

- **a. 15 minutes** (current choice) **(recommended):** safest; a link forwarded by mistake dies fast. Users who open e-mail slowly see "this link expired, send another".
- **b. 1 hour:** fewer expired-link complaints, a little less safe.
- **c. 24 hours:** matches some big apps; a leaked inbox is a leaked account for a day.
- **skip:** keep the current choice.
